# Cooling-room incident response

Original fictional training procedure. The limits below exist only for this software exercise. They do not establish safe chocolate storage, product release, or refrigeration settings.

## Incident CR-2026-0920

The training clock is fixed at 20 September 2026, 14:30 UTC. The Cooling room normally shows a warning during the noon-to-14:00 transfer period. In this scenario its temperature remains elevated after 14:00. Compare today's measurements with the previous day's same time window. Report the measurement period and UTC time explicitly.

The sample cooling-room temperature warning starts above 18 °C. This is an invented dashboard threshold. It does not establish that any batch is safe or unsafe. The quality representative owns product decisions.

## Initial assessment

The operator checks the observation time, affected room and metric. Review temperature, humidity, supply-air temperature and cooling-unit power together. A temperature reading describes a condition, not its cause. Check whether the data has gaps before summarising a trend.

Ask the shift manager to coordinate a person at the room. The application has no door-position sensor. A person must inspect the connecting door and record the observation. Do not infer an open door from temperature alone. Do not report that an inspection happened unless a record confirms it.

## Escalation

If the warning continues beyond the expected transfer period, the shift manager requests a maintenance assessment. The current duty roster identifies the on-duty role holders and contact routes. Missing contact information must be reported explicitly.

The maintenance coordinator decides whether external support is needed. Follow SOV-FAC-EXT-01 for arranging a visit. Equipment manuals supply possible checks but do not grant site access or permission to repair.

## Recovery

The shift manager records the operational decision after maintenance and quality supply the required releases. Resolving a monitoring incident requires evidence of recovery and the applicable human decision. The assistant may summarise evidence and propose next checks. It cannot release products, restart equipment or close this incident.
