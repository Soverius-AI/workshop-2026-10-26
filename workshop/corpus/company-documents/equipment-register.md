# Cooling equipment training register

## Lab equipment mapping

For this exercise, equipment COOL-01 in the Cooling room references the Danfoss Optyma Cooler manual AN378367211987en-US0101 and the Danfoss AK-RC 305W-SD controller guide BC419723137674en-000202. This is a fictional teaching association, not evidence of an installed, approved or compatible real-world assembly.

## Evidence boundaries

Manufacturer manuals describe product functions and possible fault causes. They do not establish the factory's actual configuration, current measurements or completed maintenance. Cite the relevant manual and PDF page. Distinguish PDF page numbers from numbers printed inside the document.

No installed parameter export or wiring diagram for a real site is available. Do not recommend a new setpoint or electrical change based on an assumed installation. Route equipment-specific decisions to the maintenance role.

## Sensor coverage

The simulated database includes room temperature, humidity, supply-air temperature and cooling-unit power. There is no door-position sensor. Packaging hall readings are a separate access scope. The technician assigned to V-042 has access to Cooling room readings only.
